# backup – plan

Status: discussion notes (2026-10-22, updated 2026-10-23).

## Terms: backups and drives

Decided 2026-10-25. Roles are backups, devices are drives (and the cloud); each device is named after the
role it serves:

- **primary backup**: the copy at home, on the **primary drive** (marker `.backup-destination`).
- **secondary backup**: a copy away from the laptop and the primary drive, i.e. on the **secondary drive**
  (in the office, marker `.secondary-backup-destination`) or in the **cloud** (Google Drive, the stand-in
  while the secondary drive is out of reach, or when the laptop is at high risk).

A photo is safe once it is in *a* secondary backup; texts say "on *the* secondary drive" when they mean
only that drive. Never "backup drive" alone.

## Responsibilities: backup and albumflow

Decided 2026-10-23, in detail in albumflow's `docs/plan.md` ("Responsibilities: albumflow and the backup
tool", "Purging albums from the backup drives").

- **backup is the overall backup**: `backup` is what I call, and it decides what runs when. It owns the
  restic repos (primary, secondary, cloud), their integrity checks, and `backup.pyz` for restores, which
  never needs albumflow.
- **albumflow** owns `Backup\pictures-new` on every destination (primary, secondary, and the plain copies
  in `G:\My Drive\Backup\pictures-new`) and all knowledge about albums: copying the raws of unfinished
  albums, moving them on to the secondary, the plain cloud copies, and purging.
- backup runs albumflow's CLI for the album raws (`albums.py`): `update-secondary` runs
  `albumflow update-secondary` after copying the snapshots (albumflow then removes both drives),
  `update-cloud` runs `albumflow update-cloud`. A missing or failing albumflow makes the command fail.
  albumflow never calls backup. No wrapper scripts.
- Delta mode: `backup_all_includes_update_cloud` in the settings, on while the secondary is out of reach and the
  laptop and primary are at increased risk (holidays). The cloud then doubles for the secondary: the
  restic delta plus albumflow's copies of the albums in the primary's `to-secondary`.
- Shared contract: only the drive markers and the rule "`Backup\` on a drive belongs to the tools,
  everything else is mine". Each tool defines the markers itself; a comment says the other tool uses the
  same ones (without repeating the names).
- The separation is done (2026-10-23); purging is `albumflow purge-backup`.

## Raw files of the new tree

restic excludes the .arw files below `Pictures\new` and `Pictures\new-old`, see `config.excludes()`. While
an album is being worked on, its raws are backed up as plain file copies instead. An album is finished when
it moves from `Large\Pictures\Pictures\new` to `Large\Pictures\Originals`, where restic covers everything.

`new-old` holds albums just as unfinished as those in `new`; the split is only about which ones I intend to
edit soon. So the backup drives don't distinguish them: their album trees are `<topic>\<album>`, whether
the album is in `new` or `new-old` on the laptop. albumflow so far only copies albums from `new`; it
should cover `new-old` too.

### Tree on the backup drives (done)

The tools (backup and albumflow) own `Backup\`, I own everything else on the drive, e.g. `Backup manual\`
with its own hand-written `README.md`. Only the marker file lives in the root.

    <primary>:\
      .backup-destination
      Backup\
        README.md                              generated by backup, read-only
        backup.pyz                             generated by backup, read-only
        restic-private\
        secondary-latest-snapshot.json
        pictures-new\
          to-secondary\<topic>\<album>         albumflow copies an album's raws here
          copied-to-secondary\<topic>\<album>  moved here after the copy to the secondary was verified
      Backup manual\
    <secondary>:\
      .secondary-backup-destination
      Backup\
        README.md, backup.pyz
        restic-private-secondary\
        pictures-new\<topic>\<album>

- `copied-to-secondary` on the primary: keeps the raws of the most recent albums.
- `pictures-new` on the secondary: keeps an album's raws as long as there is space. Once the album is
  finished it may go, but as late as I can afford.
- `pictures-new` is albumflow's (see above); `backup update-secondary` runs `albumflow update-secondary`.

## Done: the plain cloud copies moved to albumflow

`update-cloud` backs up only into the cloud restic repo, then runs `albumflow update-cloud`, which copies the
albums in the primary's `to-secondary` (the complete imports) to `G:\My Drive\Backup\pictures-new`. The
hand-written `delta_albums` setting is gone.

## To do: regular integrity checks of the restic repos

The primary and the secondary repo should be checked for integrity regularly and automatically, so a
damaged repo is noticed long before a restore needs it. To decide:

- What to run: `restic check` (structure only, fast), or with `--read-data-subset` (e.g. `1/12` each time,
  so all data gets read over twelve runs), or occasionally a full `--read-data`.
- When: as part of `backup-all` and `update-secondary` (the drives are connected then anyway), e.g. when
  the last check of that repo is older than some days, with the time of the last check kept next to the
  repo, like `secondary-latest-snapshot.json`.
- How a failure is reported, so it can't be overlooked.

## Moved to albumflow: making room on the drives

Decided 2026-10-23, see albumflow's plan ("Purging albums from the backup drives"): an album may be purged
from a drive once it is in `Originals`; the loss of the raws only in `new\OnlyForOthers\<album>` and of the
culled ones is accepted.
